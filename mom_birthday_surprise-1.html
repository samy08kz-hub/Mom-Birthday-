No wrapping paper. No big box. Just a little surprise waiting behind these buttons.

Dear Mom,

Happy Birthday to the woman whose love has been part of my every chapter. I may not always say everything perfectly, and I may not always show how much I notice, but I hope you know that you mean more to me than words can properly explain.

Thank you for the countless things you do that can sometimes look ordinary from the outside, but mean so much to the people who receive them. Thank you for your care, your patience, your strength, and for being there in ways that I might only fully understand as I grow older. So many of the little comforts in my life exist because of you.

I hope this new year of your life brings you peaceful mornings, genuine laughter, good health, beautiful surprises, and people who make you feel as loved as you deserve to feel. I hope you get more time for yourself, more reasons to smile, and moments where you can simply relax and enjoy life without worrying about everything else.

You deserve happiness that is not just for one birthday, but in the everyday moments too. I wish I could wrap up all the good things in the world and place them in your hands. Until then, here's a small page on the internet to remind you that someone is celebrating you today — and always.

I love you, Mom. Thank you for being you.

With all my love,
Your child ♡

🌷 A birthday wish just for you
May every year ahead be softer, brighter, healthier, and kinder to you. May the things you quietly wish for find their way to you.
You are loved. Today and every day. ♥
If I could choose one thing for you, it would be this: a life filled with the same warmth and care that you have given to others. Happy 44th Birthday, Mom! 🎂
